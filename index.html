THE QUICK EXPLAINER

How the
estimates work.

These lightweight examples use only the sample team metrics shown on each card. They demonstrate a method, not a validated prediction system.

01 / AMERICAN FOOTBALL

Scoring baseline

Expected points blend each team’s points scored with its opponent’s points allowed. A small home-field adjustment is applied, then a logistic curve converts the expected margin to win probability.

INPUTSPoints scored per gamePoints allowed per game
02 / SOCCER

Goals & draws

Expected goals combine attack and opponent-concession rates. A Poisson score model estimates home-win, draw, and away-win probabilities across possible scorelines.

INPUTSGoals scored per gameGoals conceded per game
03 / FAIR ODDS

Probability, inverted

Fair decimal odds are the inverse of estimated probability. American odds are converted from that estimate. These omit bookmaker margin and are not market quotes.

EXAMPLE50% 2.00 decimal50% +100 American

KNOW THE LIMITS

Useful estimates.
No guarantees.

A claimed 99% hit rate would require rigorous evidence across a large, independent, out-of-sample test set. This demo has no such validation and deliberately makes no accuracy claim.

Real match outcomes are uncertain. Model estimates can be wrong, sample data can be misleading, and fair odds are not bookmaker odds.
